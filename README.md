## Main Features
<a id="main-features"></a>

### Core Functionality
- **Unique Cube Mechanics** — Cubic Chess replaces traditional chess pieces with cubes, providing a 3D experience. Each cube displays the images of chess pieces, allowing for a fresh visual approach to the game.
- **Transforming Pawns** — When you eliminate a non-pawn piece, it goes into a stock that allows you to transform one of your pawns into that piece for strategic advantages, enhancing gameplay tactics.
- **Color Customization** — Players can choose their playing piece colors and board colors, allowing for a personalized gaming experience that reflects personal preferences.
- **Single and Multiplayer Modes** — Challenge the computer or engage in multiplayer matches against friends, adding variety and excitement to your chess sessions.
- **Intuitive Interface** — The game interface is user-friendly, ensuring easy navigation and a smooth gaming experience for all players.

### Advanced Capabilities

- **Visual Representation of Pieces** — The top of each cube displays the value of the piece, ensuring players can easily recognize their options during the game.
